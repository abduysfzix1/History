Moments that mattered

A brief timeline

356 BCE

Birth of Alexander

Alexander was born in Pella, capital of ancient Macedon.

1876

Birth of Muhammad Ali Jinnah

Jinnah was born in Karachi, then part of British India.

1877

Birth of Allama Iqbal

Muhammad Iqbal was born in Sialkot and became a leading poet and thinker.

1930

Allahabad Address

Iqbal delivered his presidential address at the All-India Muslim League session.

1947

Independence of Pakistan

Pakistan became independent on 14 August 1947, with Jinnah as its first Governor-General.
